## The short answer
Give a direct answer, including the cheapest adequate option and key limitations.

## Who is this for?
Define use cases, constraints and exclusions.

## What you actually need
Minimum and recommended hardware/software requirements; cite primary sources.

## Options compared
Compare new, used/refurbished and already-owned hardware. Explain price date, region, shipping/tax assumptions and total cost.

## Setup / methodology
Reproducible steps or testing method; never imply hands-on work if none occurred.

## What works and what doesn't
Compatibility, limitations, security, maintenance and power usage.

## Budget alternatives
Identify less expensive adequate options, with trade-offs.

## Do you actually need to buy anything?
Describe reuse and no-purchase approaches.

## Our assessment
Independent verdict: best value, best overall where justified, and who should skip it.

## Testing status
Explicitly separate measured results, vendor claims, and unverified assumptions.

## Sources and related reading
Primary documentation, date checked, relevant internal links.
