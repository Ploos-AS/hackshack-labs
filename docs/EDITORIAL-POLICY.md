# HackShack Labs — Editorial independence policy

**Best value. No brand loyalty. No unnecessary upgrades.**

HackShack Labs is published by Ploos AS. Our editorial recommendations are independent of manufacturers, distributors, advertisers and affiliate programs.

## How we recommend
- Recommend the lowest-cost option that adequately meets the stated requirements, or the best overall option when meaningful benefits justify the price. Explain the trade-off.
- Consider used, refurbished, repairable and already-owned equipment alongside new products. Never assume an upgrade is necessary.
- Evaluate actual functionality, price/performance, reliability, power consumption and total cost of ownership, Linux/open-source compatibility, repairability, security updates and expected useful life.
- Compare competing brands on equal terms. No manufacturer receives preferential treatment because of a commercial relationship.
- State the region, currency, date and assumptions behind prices. English-language articles must not assume shipping or taxes to Norway.
- Disclose affiliate links and any loaned/review hardware prominently. Affiliate commissions do not determine rankings, conclusions or coverage.
- Separate **hands-on tested**, **documentation-based**, and **not hardware verified** claims. Never fabricate benchmarks, measurements or firsthand experience.
- Explain important drawbacks and practical alternatives; include a **Do you actually need to buy anything?** section where relevant.
- Keep sponsored content visibly identified and editorially separate. Correct errors openly.

**Old hardware deserves a second life, not a premium price.**

## Review checklist
1. What problem does this solve, and for whom?
2. What is the cheapest adequate solution, including existing hardware?
3. What does spending more actually improve?
4. What is confirmed by testing versus vendor documentation?
5. Are pricing geography, disclosures and limitations clear?
6. Would we reach the same recommendation without an affiliate program?

This policy applies to English originals and Norwegian Bokmål translations.
