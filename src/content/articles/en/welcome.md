HackShack Labs documents practical computing projects, hardware experiments, and reproducible technical guides.

Our offline-networking series documents small routers, OpenWrt and planned MikroWrt/Ploos PocketBox work. **PirateBox and LibraryBox are discontinued historical projects**, not planned new installation guides. Hardware instructions will be labelled verified only after firmware and device revisions have been checked on physical equipment. See the [Offline Networking series](/series/offline-networking/).

## Explore published guides

Interested in small, repairable infrastructure? Start with [why mini PCs make good homelab servers](/articles/why-mini-pcs-make-excellent-homelab-servers/) and our [used mini PC buying cautions](/articles/used-mini-pc-price-trap/). For privacy-focused services, compare [Proton Mail and self-hosted email](/articles/proton-mail-vs-self-hosted-email/). Radio enthusiasts can visit [our amateur radio and SDR introduction](/articles/welcome-to-the-radio-shack/).

We distinguish documentation-based articles from hands-on testing, and mark affiliate links when present. A published guide is not automatically a hardware-verified result.
